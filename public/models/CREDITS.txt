The character is an adult woman in casual clothes (sweater, denim skirt, shoes).
The original export is a T-pose. The app rotates the upper arms down for the rest pose.
Lip sync uses the jaw bone (Bip01 MJaw). The facial blendshape export was not used,
because the converter dropped the viseme names.

three.js (MIT) is vendored under public/vendor/three and loaded from this origin.
No CDN at runtime.

Likeness check (not shipped). The reference portrait needs long honey-blonde wavy hair past the shoulders, green eyes, a black ribbed V-neck tank, and blue jeans. Recoloring the current mesh cannot do that: the hair cards stay short, and the clothes stay a sweater and a denim skirt.

Microsoft Rocketbox adults Female_Adult_01 through Female_Adult_17 were reviewed from the MIT previews (copyright 2020 Microsoft, same LICENSE.md). None combine long wavy blonde hair with a sleeveless tank and jeans. The nearest are Female_Adult_08 (long straight brown hair, graphic t-shirt, jeans) and Female_Adult_01 (blonde ponytail, pink shirt, jeans). A recolor would leave straight hair and sleeves, so it would not read as the portrait.

Not used, and not copied into this repo:
- ToxSam 100Avatars (stated CC0) are stylized VRM characters, not this woman.
- Reine (Melnus) is CC-BY now, previously CC-BY-NC-SA, and is an anime model.
- Mixamo characters were not downloaded. Their terms do not grant a clean right to redistribute the raw model here.
- No NC or unknown-license file was added.

What would unblock a swap: one adult woman glTF or VRM, CC0 or MIT or CC-BY, with separate long wavy hair, a sleeveless top, and jeans, on a humanoid skeleton the current idle and wave can drive. Until that file exists, Prova stays Female Adult 02.
